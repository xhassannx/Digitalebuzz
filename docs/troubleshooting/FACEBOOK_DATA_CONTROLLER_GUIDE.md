# Facebook App Review - Data Controller Guide

Guide for answering the "Data Controller" question in Facebook App Review.

---

## 🎯 Question: Who is the Data Controller?

**Question:**
> "Who is the person or entity that will be responsible for all Platform Data Meta shares with you?"

**What They're Asking:**
- Who legally owns/controls your app?
- Who is responsible for data handling?
- What legal entity controls the Platform Data?
- This is the "data controller" (GDPR term) or "data owner"

---

## ✅ Answer Format

### For Business/Company:

**Answer:** Your company's legal name

**Examples:**
- "ABC Technologies Private Limited"
- "XYZ Inc."
- "Your Company Name LLC"
- "Your Business Name"

**Requirements:**
- ✅ Must match your Terms of Service
- ✅ Must match your Privacy Policy
- ✅ Must be the legal entity name (not trading name)
- ✅ Should be registered business name

---

### For Individual/Sole Proprietor:

**Answer:** Your full legal name

**Examples:**
- "John Doe"
- "Jane Smith"

**Requirements:**
- ✅ Full legal name (not nickname)
- ✅ Must match legal documents
- ✅ Must match Terms of Service

---

## 📋 How to Determine Your Data Controller

### Check These Documents:

1. **Terms of Service:**
   - Look for "Company Name" or "We/Us"
   - Usually at the top or bottom of the page
   - Example: "ABC Technologies ('we', 'us', 'our')"

2. **Privacy Policy:**
   - Look for "Data Controller" or "We"
   - Usually mentions who controls data
   - Example: "ABC Technologies is the data controller..."

3. **Business Registration:**
   - Check your business registration documents
   - Use the exact legal entity name
   - Match incorporation documents

4. **Domain Registration:**
   - Check WHOIS information
   - See who owns the domain
   - May indicate the legal entity

## ⚠️ Important Requirements

### Must Match:

- ✅ **Terms of Service** - Same entity name
- ✅ **Privacy Policy** - Same entity name
- ✅ **Business Registration** - Exact legal name
- ✅ **App Settings** - Same name in Meta App settings

### Common Mistakes:

- ❌ Using trading name instead of legal name
- ❌ Using personal name when you have a company
- ❌ Not matching Terms of Service
- ❌ Using abbreviated names
- ❌ Using DBA (Doing Business As) names

---

## 🔍 How to Find Your Legal Entity Name

### If You Have a Company:

1. **Check Business Registration:**
   - Look at incorporation documents
   - Check business registration certificate
   - Verify with business registrar

2. **Check Legal Documents:**
   - Terms of Service
   - Privacy Policy
   - Business contracts
   - Tax documents

3. **Check Meta App Settings:**
   - Go to App Dashboard → Settings → Basic
   - See "App Name" and "App Contact Email"
   - Use the legal entity behind these

### If You're Individual:

1. **Use Full Legal Name:**
   - As it appears on government ID
   - As it appears on legal documents
   - Full name (first + last)
