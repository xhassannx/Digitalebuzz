# Facebook App Review - National Security Requests Guide

Guide for answering the "National Security Requests" question in Facebook App Review.

---

## 🎯 Question: National Security Requests

**Question:**
> "Have you provided the personal data or personal information of users to public authorities in response to national security requests in the past 12 months?"

**Important Note:**
> "This does not include requests related to search warrants or court orders associated with criminal investigations."

---

## ✅ Recommended Answer: **NO** ✅

**Select: "No"**

**Why:** For most SaaS platforms, especially new or small businesses, you likely haven't received any national security requests.

---

## 📋 Understanding the Question

### What They're Asking:

**National Security Requests:**
- Requests from government agencies for national security purposes
- NOT regular law enforcement requests
- NOT search warrants or court orders
- NOT criminal investigation requests
- Examples: FISA orders, National Security Letters (US), similar requests in other countries

### What They're NOT Asking:

❌ **Regular Law Enforcement:**
- Search warrants
- Court orders
- Criminal investigation requests
- These are EXCLUDED from this question

❌ **Legal Compliance:**
- Subpoenas
- Legal discovery
- Regulatory compliance
- These are separate

---

## 🎯 When to Answer "No"

### Answer "No" If:

✅ **You're a new/small business:**
- Just starting out
- Haven't received government requests
- No national security requests received

✅ **You haven't received requests:**
- No national security letters
- No FISA orders
- No similar requests

✅ **You only received regular legal requests:**
- Search warrants (excluded)
- Court orders (excluded)
- Criminal investigation requests (excluded)

✅ **Most Common Scenario:**
- 99% of SaaS platforms answer "No"
- National security requests are rare
- Usually only large tech companies receive them

---

## ⚠️ When to Answer "Yes"

### Answer "Yes" If:

⚠️ **You've received national security requests:**
- FISA orders (US)
- National Security Letters (US)
- Similar requests in your country
- In the past 12 months

⚠️ **You're legally required to share:**
- Government requested data for national security
- You complied with the request
- It involved Meta Platform Data

**Note:** This is very rare for small/medium businesses.

---

## 🚫 When to Answer "Prohibited by Law"

### Answer "Prohibited by Law" If:

🚫 **You're legally prohibited from disclosing:**
- Your country's laws prevent you from answering
- Company policy prevents disclosure
- You have a gag order preventing disclosure

**Note:** This is also rare. Most businesses can answer "No" or "Yes".

## 📝 Complete Answer Template

### Standard Answer:

**Question:** Have you provided personal data to public authorities in response to national security requests?

**Answer:** **No** ✅

**Explanation (if requested):**
```
We have not received any national security requests from public authorities in the past 12 months. We have not provided any Meta Platform Data to public authorities for national security purposes.

Any legal requests we may have received (such as search warrants or court orders for criminal investigations) are excluded from this question as specified.
```

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### What Counts as National Security Requests:

✅ **Counts:**
- FISA orders (US)
- National Security Letters (US)
- Similar national security requests in other countries
- Requests from intelligence agencies
- Requests for national security purposes

❌ **Doesn't Count:**
- Search warrants (excluded)
- Court orders (excluded)
- Criminal investigation requests (excluded)
- Regular law enforcement requests
- Subpoenas for civil cases

### Time Period:

- **Past 12 months** only
- Don't count requests older than 12 months
- Count from today backwards 12 months
