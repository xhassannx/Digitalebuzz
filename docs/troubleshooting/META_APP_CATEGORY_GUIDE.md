# Meta App Category Selection Guide

Guide for selecting the correct app category when creating a Meta app for Facebook Lead Ads and WhatsApp Business integrations.

---

## 🎯 Recommended App Category

### **Primary Choice: "Business" or "Business and Pages"** ✅

**Select: "Business"** (or **"Business and Pages"** if available) as your app category.

**Why "Business" is Best:**
- ✅ Designed for business tools and SaaS platforms
- ✅ Supports Lead Ads integrations
- ✅ Supports WhatsApp Business API
- ✅ Appropriate for multi-tenant SaaS applications
- ✅ Best fit for CRM and contact management tools
- ✅ Allows both Lead Ads and WhatsApp permissions
- ✅ Specifically designed for managing business assets (Pages, Ads, Insights)

---

## 📋 App Category Options

### Option 1: **Business** ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**

**Best For:**
- SaaS platforms
- Business management tools
- CRM integrations
- Lead management systems
- Multi-tenant applications
- **Your Use Case**: ✅ Perfect fit

**Supports:**
- ✅ Facebook Lead Ads
- ✅ WhatsApp Business API
- ✅ Business Manager integrations
- ✅ Page management
- ✅ Lead retrieval permissions

**Why Choose This:**
- Most appropriate for your SaaS platform
- Supports all permissions you need
- Common category for similar apps
- Best for App Review approval

---

### Option 2: **Marketing** ⚠️ Alternative

**Best For:**
- Marketing automation tools
- Lead generation platforms
- Ad management tools

**Supports:**
- ✅ Facebook Lead Ads
- ✅ Marketing API
- ⚠️ WhatsApp Business (may be limited)

**Why Consider:**
- Good if you focus heavily on Lead Ads
- May have limitations for WhatsApp Business

**Recommendation:** Use only if "Business" is not available or if you're primarily a marketing tool.

---

### Option 3: **Communication** ⚠️ Alternative

**Best For:**
- Messaging platforms
- Communication tools
- Chat applications

**Supports:**
- ✅ WhatsApp Business API
- ⚠️ Lead Ads (may be limited)

**Why Consider:**
- Good if you focus heavily on WhatsApp messaging
- May have limitations for Lead Ads

**Recommendation:** Use only if "Business" is not available or if you're primarily a messaging tool.

---

## 🎯 Final Recommendation

### **Select: "Business"** ✅

**Step-by-Step:**

1. Go to Meta for Developers → Create App
2. When asked for **App Type**, select:
   - **"Business"** ✅
   
3. Fill in app details:
   - **App Name**: Your platform name
   - **App Contact Email**: Your business email
   - **Business Account**: Select your Business Manager account

4. **Category**: "Business" will be automatically set

## 📋 App Creation Steps

### Step 1: Create App
1. Go to [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com/)
2. Click **"My Apps"** → **"Create App"**

### Step 2: Select App Type
- **Select: "Business"** ✅
- **OR** **"Business and Pages"** ✅ (if this option is available)

### Step 3: Fill App Details
- **App Name**: [Your Platform Name]
- **App Contact Email**: [Your Email]
- **Business Account**: [Select Your Business Manager]

### Step 4: Add Products
- **Facebook Login** (for OAuth)
- **WhatsApp** (for WhatsApp Business API)

### Step 5: Configure Settings
- OAuth redirect URIs
- App domains
- Privacy Policy URL
- Terms of Service URL

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Category Cannot Be Changed Later:
- ⚠️ App category is set during creation
- ⚠️ Cannot be changed after app is created
- ✅ Choose carefully - "Business" is the right choice

### If "Business" Not Available:
- Check if you need Business Manager account
- Some categories require Business verification
- Contact Meta support if needed
